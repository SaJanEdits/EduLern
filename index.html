Learn anywhere. Learn anytime.

📚 What is EduLearn?

EduLearn is an online educational platform designed to help students access useful learning videos organized by grade and subject.

Students can select their grade, choose a subject, and watch available lessons directly through YouTube.

The platform includes Mathematics, Science, ICT, English, History and Sinhala content.

EduLearn is designed with a simple and student-friendly interface so learning resources are easy to find.
